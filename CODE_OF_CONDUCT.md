# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the safe-npm community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- being respectful and constructive in technical discussions;
- giving and receiving feedback based on code, evidence and documented behavior;
- acknowledging different experience levels and viewpoints;
- keeping security discussions responsible and avoiding unnecessary disclosure of sensitive information;
- focusing disagreements on technical trade-offs rather than individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information without permission, or intentionally disrupting project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately rather than opening a public issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant, and is intentionally kept concise for this project.
